Client information
Payment arrangement

Person responsible for the account

This person must complete and accept the payment agreement. For a child or sponsored client, enter the responsible adult’s details.

The supplied payment form requires a signed sponsor letter stating the number of sessions, expectations and payment commitment. The practice must review it.

Medical-aid details

Complete these for direct claims or when claiming back a private payment. Cover and payment are not guaranteed.

The supplied payment form states that the medical aid will not fund a claim without the required code. Please choose private payment or contact the practice to discuss this before proceeding.

Client consent
Read client-consent information

The supplied form covers the nature of counselling, confidentiality and its limits, supervision, session records, counsellor and client responsibilities, and appointments. Sessions last 45–60 minutes; the form describes a typical course of 10 sessions. Less than 24 hours’ cancellation notice incurs the full session fee. Confidentiality exceptions include serious risk of harm, a subpoena, illegal activities, and harm or neglect involving children or vulnerable adults. Please read the full document and ask the practice about anything unclear.

Read the complete client consent form (PDF, opens separately)
Parental / legal guardian consent

Required for child / teenager sessions, a client under 18, or a family booking that includes a minor. The practice must review guardian authority and any additional signatures required. No counsellor signature is created by this website.

Read parental-consent information

The Child Consent Form includes the client’s name and date of birth, confidentiality and its limits, counsellor and guardian responsibilities, session arrangements, supervision and secure session records. Its acknowledgement confirms legal guardianship, understanding of the form and permission for the named child to be seen by the appointed counsellor.

Read the complete parental consent form (PDF, opens separately)
Payment consent and practice terms
Read payment, medical-aid and cancellation terms

The person responsible for the account remains liable for medical-aid shortfalls and rejected claims. Missed appointments, late cancellations and rescheduling with less than 24 hours’ notice incur the full applicable fee and are the account holder’s responsibility. Consultation fees are due on the day of consultation under the supplied agreement. Outstanding shortfalls must be settled within 7 days of notification. The full agreement also covers claim codes, pre-authorisation, third-party sponsors, reports, refunds / account credits and debt recovery.
