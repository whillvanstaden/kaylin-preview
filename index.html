How I Can Help

Counselling offers space to talk through what is difficult and work on practical ways to cope.

  • Anxiety and worry
  • Stress and overwhelm
  • Low mood and emotional wellbeing
  • Relationship difficulties
  • Grief and life changes
  • Self-esteem and coping
  • Children and teenagers
  • Family concerns

Short-term counselling and psychological support are provided within the professional scope and competencies of an HPCSA Registered Counsellor, with referral where a different or more specialised level of care is appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a session?

Sessions are approximately 45–60 minutes.

What are the session fees?

Individual and child/teen sessions are R600. Couples/family sessions are R650. Fees are the same for online and in-person counselling.

Do you offer online counselling?

Yes. Online counselling is available within South Africa, subject to suitability and practice arrangements.

What happens at the first session?

The first session is used to understand what has brought you to counselling, discuss your needs and goals, and decide on an appropriate way forward.

Is counselling confidential?

Counselling is handled confidentially within applicable ethical and legal requirements. Relevant limitations to confidentiality can be explained before or during the counselling process.

What if I am unsure whether Kaylin is the right practitioner for me?

You are welcome to contact the practice with a basic suitability question. Where a different level or type of care is required, an appropriate referral may be recommended.
