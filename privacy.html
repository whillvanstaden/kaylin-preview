Kaylin Website Bookings — Google Calendar privacy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Kaylin Website Bookings is the appointment-scheduling connection for Kaylin Nel Counselling. The practice Google account authorises the calendar connection. Clients do not need to connect their own Google accounts to request an appointment through the website.

Google data and permissions

The connection checks the authorised Google account's verified email address to confirm that it belongs to the practice. It reads calendar busy times to work out which appointment times are available. Visitors see available appointment slots, not the details of other calendar events.

Google's event permission allows the connection to see, create, change and delete events on calendars owned by the authorised account. The booking system uses that permission to create its appointment events, check those events, move an appointment when rescheduling, and remove an appointment when cancelling. The connection does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files or Google Contacts.

Appointment events contain a general appointment title, the appointment date and time, and the attendee email address used for the invitation. Client intake, consent and medical-aid form answers are not added to Google Calendar events. Google sends the invitation and appointment updates to the attendee through its calendar service.

Use, storage and service providers

Google data is used to operate the practice's scheduling and calendar connection. It is not sold or used by this application for advertising or AI-model training.

The website backend runs on Cloudflare. The authorisation credential that lets it reconnect to Google is stored encrypted in the backend database and is not included in public webpages. Temporary access credentials are used on the server when contacting Google. Google processes the calendar events and invitations, and Cloudflare processes the connection and scheduling data as the website's infrastructure provider. Access to connection data is limited to authorised practice and technical administrators for operation and support.

The practice's public booking and payment flow has not yet been activated. Private checks use fictitious appointment details. A private live-payment check is handled by Yoco; payment-card details are entered on Yoco's payment page and are not stored by this website.

Disconnecting and deletion requests

The practice account holder can stop future Google access by removing the application's access in their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Saved connection credentials remain in the backend until an authorised administrator replaces or removes them. Revoking access does not itself delete existing Google Calendar events or booking records.

To request removal of the saved calendar connection, or ask about information held by the practice, contact admin@knelpractice.com. Removal of the connection stops the website from checking that calendar or managing its appointments until it is connected again.

Google's data-use requirements

Kaylin Website Bookings' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Website privacy information

The booking preview does not transmit or save the information entered. For questions about personal information held by the practice, contact admin@knelpractice.com. Please avoid sending sensitive clinical information by ordinary email.

Booking, payment and cancellation terms

Online booking and payment are not yet available. Contact the practice to arrange an appointment and ask about applicable cancellation, rescheduling and refund conditions before booking.

Resource-store terms

Resources are in development and are not available to buy or download. Usage, delivery and purchase terms will be provided before sales open.
