Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a session?

Sessions are approximately 45–60 minutes.

What are the session fees?

Individual and child/teen sessions are R600. Couples/family sessions are R650. Fees are the same for online and in-person counselling.

Do you offer online counselling?

Yes. Online counselling is available within South Africa, subject to suitability and practice arrangements.

Is counselling confidential?

Counselling is handled confidentially within applicable ethical and legal requirements. Relevant limitations to confidentiality are explained before and during the counselling process.

What if I am unsure whether Kaylin is the right practitioner for me?

You are welcome to contact the practice with a basic suitability question. Where a different level or type of care is required, an appropriate referral may be recommended.

You can also look out for our upcoming blog post, “Starting Your Healing Journey,” which offers guidance on selecting the right mental health practitioner for you.
