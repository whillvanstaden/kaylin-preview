Professional online counselling from a private and suitable space.

You will need reliable internet, privacy and a suitable device. Availability is subject to suitability and practice arrangements.

This form of counselling is often used for convenience, accessibility, and flexibility, especially for individuals who might not have easy access to in-person counselling sessions due to geographic, time, or mobility constraints.

Your first session

What happens at the first session?

Every counselling journey begins with understanding. During our first session, we’ll talk through your concerns, explore your hopes for counselling, and decide together on the most appropriate way forward.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a session?

Sessions are approximately 45–60 minutes.

What are the session fees?

Individual and child/teen sessions are R600. Couples/family sessions are R650. Fees are the same for online and in-person counselling.

Do you offer online counselling?

Yes. Online counselling is available within South Africa, subject to suitability and practice arrangements.

Is counselling confidential?

Counselling is handled confidentially within applicable ethical and legal requirements. Relevant limitations to confidentiality are explained before and during the counselling process.

What if I am unsure whether Kaylin is the right practitioner for me?

You are welcome to contact the practice with a basic suitability question. Where a different level or type of care is required, an appropriate referral may be recommended.

You can also look out for our upcoming blog post, “Starting Your Healing Journey,” which offers guidance on selecting the right mental health practitioner for you.

Client consent
Read client-consent information

The supplied form covers the nature of counselling, confidentiality and its limits, supervision, session records, counsellor and client responsibilities, and appointments. Sessions last 45–60 minutes; the form describes a typical course of 10 sessions. Less than 24 hours’ cancellation notice incurs the full session fee. Confidentiality exceptions include serious risk of harm, a subpoena, illegal activities, and harm or neglect involving children or vulnerable adults. Please read the full document and ask the practice about anything unclear.

Parental / legal guardian consent
